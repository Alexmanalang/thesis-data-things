This undergraduate thesis examines how Artificial Intelligence is currently being used and integrated within architectural design and production workflows, and how its role may be appropriately defined within professional architectural practice.

The study focuses on the relationship between AI applications, architectural workflow tasks, and professional responsibility, with particular attention to the Philippine context and the workflow realities of architects practicing in Cavite.

Participation will help provide evidence on how AI is being used in practice, the conditions surrounding its integration, and how AI-assisted systems function within architectural workflows.

This is an independent academic thesis. It is not a survey issued by the United Architects of the Philippines (UAP) or any professional body.

Who Can Participate?

Cavite-Based Architects

The survey is intended for architects who:

  • Are Registered and Licensed Architects in the Philippines;
  • Are currently practicing or residing in Cavite Province;
  • Are actively involved in architectural workflows, including design development, technical documentation, coordination, or related production activities; and
  • Have exposure to digital tools and varying levels of experience with AI-assisted technologies.
You do not need to be an advanced AI user. Different levels of familiarity and use are relevant to understanding the conditions surrounding AI integration in architectural practice.

Subject Matter Experts

The study also invites professionals with specialized expertise at the intersection of AI and architectural design and/or production in the Philippines.

SME eligibility is based on documented professional experience demonstrating relevant expertise, evidenced through one or more of the following:

  • Professional talks, lectures, panels, or workshops;
  • Publications or research;
  • Documented architectural projects involving AI applications; or
  • Company or studio work specializing in AI-applied architectural technologies.

SMEs may be based anywhere in the Philippines and do not need to be located in Cavite.

Participants must either be a Registered and Licensed Architect or occupy an architecture-related professional role relevant to AI, architectural technology, digital practice, or related fields.

What Will I Be Asked to Do?

For Cavite-Based Architects

You will be asked to complete a structured survey concerning your experience with AI and digital technologies in architectural practice. The survey covers areas such as:

AI familiarity and useYour level of exposure to and use of AI-assisted systems.
Architectural workflowsWhere AI is currently used or considered within design, visualization, documentation, production, coordination, and related activities.
Integration conditionsFactors such as technological readiness, workflow compatibility, organizational conditions, and practical barriers.
Professional practiceExperiences and perceptions concerning AI-assisted work, review, validation, and professional responsibility.

For Subject Matter Experts

You will be invited to participate in a semi-structured interview concerning your experience and expertise in AI-assisted architectural practice. Discussion areas include:

AI applicationsHow AI is being applied within architectural design and production.
Workflow useHow AI-assisted systems function within actual architectural workflows.
Implementation conditionsConditions, opportunities, limitations, and practical considerations affecting AI integration.
Human–AI collaborationHow architects and AI-assisted systems interact within professional workflows.

Why Participate?

Your participation contributes to a study examining AI not simply as a technological tool, but as something increasingly incorporated into architectural work. The study seeks to understand:

What is AI doing within architectural workflows?
What conditions shape its integration?
What professional considerations affect how much AI involvement is appropriate?

The findings will contribute to the development of a Strategic Human–AI Architectural Workflow Framework intended to provide a structured basis for considering AI roles, degree of involvement, and appropriate architect oversight across architectural workflow tasks.

What Happens to My Information?

Information gathered through the study will be used for academic research purposes and analyzed in relation to the study's research objectives.

Participation is voluntary. Participants will be provided with the appropriate informed-consent information before taking part.
